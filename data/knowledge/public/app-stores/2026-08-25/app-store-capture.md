## Use limitation

Ratings and downloads are public store-page observations at a specified store locale. They do not establish country-specific active users, installs, market share, sales, retention, conversion, revenue, CPC, CPA, CVR, ROAS, or campaign success. Header review counts and detailed-section review counts are preserved separately and must not be averaged or reconciled into a new metric. Public review text was not used as statistical evidence.
