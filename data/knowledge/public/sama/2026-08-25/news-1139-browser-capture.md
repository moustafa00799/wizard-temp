The page states that electronic payments accounted for 85% of total retail payments in 2025, up from 79% in 2024. It also states that the number of electronic transactions reached 14.6 billion in 2025, compared with 12.6 billion in 2024. The page attributes the growth to national payment systems, including mada, POS and ecommerce payments, alongside other national payment systems.
