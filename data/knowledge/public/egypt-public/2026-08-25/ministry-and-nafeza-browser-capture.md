# Egyptian public-data alternatives capture

## Ministry of Planning and Economic Development — National Accounts

- Source URL: https://mped.gov.eg/Analytics?lang=en
- Publisher: Egypt Ministry of Planning and Economic Development
- Capture date: 2026-08-25

The public page says that the interactive national-accounts datasets provide annual and quarterly GDP according to expenditure elements and economic activities, GDP and investment growth rates, and regional accounts with economic data by economic region and governorate. It links to GDP search, public-investment search, and GDP-by-governorate search pages. The page was used as a dataset-discovery/context source; no numeric row was copied because the linked interactive tables were not part of the captured text.

## Nafeza — foreign-exchange conversion page

- Source URL: https://sandbox.nafeza.gov.eg/ar/currencies
- Publisher shown on page: Egyptian Customs Authority; public service presented by Nafeza
- Displayed rate date: 24 Feb 2026
- Capture date: 2026-08-25

The public page displayed customs conversion rates against the Egyptian pound, including USD 47.8800 EGP and SAR 12.7646 EGP for the displayed date. These are customs conversion rates, not consumer market prices or advertising benchmarks. They may be used only for explicit currency normalization while retaining the displayed date and source.

## Scope limitation

Neither page establishes audience size, ecommerce demand, product supply, market share, customer acquisition, conversion, CPC, CPA, CVR, ROAS, reach, frequency, saturation, or competitor performance. The data.gov.eg and Egypt Data Portal routes were not treated as successful datasets when they returned rejection/error states.
